◆ kMaxDiagLength

constexpr size_t kMaxDiagLength = 256
staticconstexpr

Upper bound on the characters a diagnostic renders for an LLVM entity.

One rendering is unbounded by nature: a GlobalVariable prints its entire initializer, and a node with long MDStrings or large inline constants prints them whole. On a merged library a single unbounded diagnostic reaches tens of megabytes, so every rendering path below truncates its output. Diagnostics only need to identify the entity; the full form is in the input being imported.
